# Foot Repose Staff Roster
Weekly staff roster for Foot Repose: shift times, custom and split shifts, OFF, PENDING OFF, SICK LEAVE and VACATION (shown in red bold with yellow highlight on screen, in print and in the A4 PDF), PDO and overtime, notes, saved weeks, backups, CSV export and installable app.

Add staff with **Edit staff** and timings with **Edit shift options**. Rosters stay on each device and are not sent anywhere.
